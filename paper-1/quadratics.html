1.1 Quadratics

Completing the square · discriminant · equations and inequalities · simultaneous equations

CIE 9709 Paper 1 Pure Mathematics 1, syllabus 1.1.
重要本节考纲要求

本页依据 9709 Mathematics 2026–2027 syllabus 1.1 Quadratics：

  • 对 (ax^2+bx+c) completing the square，并使用 completed-square form；
  • 求 quadratic polynomial 的 discriminant，并判断 real roots、distinct roots 或 repeated root；
  • 解 quadratic equations 与 quadratic inequalities；
  • 用 substitution 解一个 linear equation 与一个 quadratic equation 组成的 simultaneous equations；
  • 识别并解在某个 function of (x) 中为 quadratic 的方程。

下列 20 组题目均来自工作区保存的 Paper 1 原始 past-paper PDF。题干只把 PDF 排版符号整理为 LaTeX，没有改写题目或补造题目；答案按对应 mark scheme 的得分步骤展开。

1. Core knowledge

Completing the square

对 (ax^2+bx+c)，先提出 (a)：

ax^2+bx+c=a\left(x^2+\frac ba x\right)+c =a\left(x+\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2+c-\frac{b^2}{4a}.

因此顶点为

\left(-\frac{b}{2a},\ c-\frac{b^2}{4a}\right).

当 (a>0) 时是 minimum；当 (a<0) 时是 maximum。写出平方形式后，最值和 range 通常可以直接读出。

Worked example · 原始笔记单卡片

这张笔记例题连接本节的 completing the square、vertex 和 maximum value：先把二次式写成 (-a(x-b)^2+c)，再由平方项的符号判断最大值。

Discriminant

对

ax^2+bx+c=0,

定义

\Delta=b^2-4ac.

当 (a,b,cR) 时：

\Delta>0\Rightarrow\text{two distinct real roots},\qquad \Delta=0\Rightarrow\text{one repeated real root},\qquad \Delta<0\Rightarrow\text{no real roots}.

若题目问 line 与 quadratic curve 有 two distinct intersections，先令两条曲线的 (y) 相等，整理成关于 (x) 的 quadratic，再使用 (Delta>0)。问 tangent 则使用 (Delta=0)。

Solving equations and inequalities

Quadratic equation 可以 factorise、complete the square 或使用 quadratic formula。Quadratic inequality 要先找 roots，再用 leading coefficient 判断区间符号；如果题目给出 parameter，边界通常来自 (Delta=0)。

对于 simultaneous equations，若一个方程是 linear，先将它写成 (y=) 或 (x=)，代入另一个 equation，得到一个 quadratic；解出后必须代回 linear equation 求另一变量。

若方程在 (x3)、(x2)、((2x-3)^2) 等整体中呈二次形式，令该整体为 (u)，先解 (u) 的 quadratic，再回代并检查原变量的定义域。

Question 1 · 9709/13/M/J/20 Q1

Find the set of values of (m) for which the line with equation (y=mx+1) and the curve with equation

y=3x^2+2x+4

intersect at two distinct points. [4]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

At an intersection,

3x^2+2x+4=mx+1,

so

3x^2+(2-m)x+3=0.

For two distinct intersection points, this quadratic must have two distinct real roots:

(2-m)^2-4(3)(3)>0.

Hence

(2-m)^2>36 \Longleftrightarrow |2-m|>6.

Therefore

\boxed{m<-4\text{ or }m>8}.

Question 4 · 9709/12/O/N/20 Q3

The equation of a curve is (y=2x^2+m(2x+1)), where (m) is a constant, and the equation of a line is (y=6x+4).

Show that, for all values of (m), the line intersects the curve at two distinct points. [5]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Equating the two expressions for (y),

2x^2+m(2x+1)=6x+4,

which gives

2x^2+(2m-6)x+(m-4)=0.

Its discriminant is

\begin{aligned} \Delta&=(2m-6)^2-4(2)(m-4)\\ &=4m^2-32m+68\\ &=4\bigl((m-4)^2+1\bigr). \end{aligned}

Since ((m-4)^2+1>0) for every real (m), (Delta>0) for every real (m). Hence the quadratic has two distinct real roots, so the line intersects the curve at two distinct points for all (m).

Question 5 · 9709/12/F/M/21 Q2

By using a suitable substitution, solve the equation

(2x-3)^2-\frac{4}{(2x-3)^2}-3=0.

[4]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Let

u=(2x-3)^2.

Then (u>0), and the equation becomes

u-\frac4u-3=0.

Multiplying by (u),

u^2-3u-4=0=(u-4)(u+1).

Because (u>0), reject (u=-1), so (u=4). Therefore

(2x-3)^2=4 \Longrightarrow 2x-3=\pm2.

Hence

\boxed{x=\frac12\text{ or }x=\frac52}.

Find the set of values of (k) for which the line and curve have two distinct points of intersection. [5]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

At an intersection,

x^2+kx+6=3x+k,

For two distinct intersections,

\begin{aligned} \Delta&=(k-3)^2-4(6-k)>0\\ &=k^2-2k-15>0\\ &=(k-5)(k+3)>0. \end{aligned}

Therefore

\boxed{k<-3\text{ or }k>5}.

x^2+2cx+4=4x+c,

Question 13 · 9709/12/F/M/23 Q1

A line has equation (y=3x-2k) and a curve has equation (y=x^2-kx+2), where (k) is a constant.

Show that the line and the curve meet for all values of (k). [4]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

At an intersection,

x^2-kx+2=3x-2k,

so

x^2-(k+3)x+(2+2k)=0.

Its discriminant is

\begin{aligned} \Delta&=(k+3)^2-4(2+2k)\\ &=k^2-2k+1\\ &=(k-1)^2\ge0. \end{aligned}

Thus there are real values of (x) for every real (k), so the line and curve meet for all values of (k). When (k=1), (Delta=0) and the intersection is a repeated point; for all other (k), (Delta>0).
