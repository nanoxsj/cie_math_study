1.7 Differentiation

Derivatives · tangents and normals · stationary points · rates of change

CIE 9709 Paper 1 Pure Mathematics 1, syllabus 1.7.
重要本节考纲要求

本页依据 9709 Mathematics 2026–2027 syllabus 1.7 Differentiation：

  • 理解 gradient、first derivative 和 second derivative；
  • 对 rational powers 使用 power rule、constant multiples、sums、differences 和 chain rule；
  • 应用 differentiation 求 gradients、tangents、normals、increasing/decreasing intervals 和 rates of change；
  • 找 stationary points，并用 second derivative 判断 maximum 或 minimum。

下列 15 组题目均来自工作区保存的 Paper 1 原始 past-paper PDF。题干只把 PDF 排版符号整理为 LaTeX，没有改写或编造题目；答案按对应 mark scheme 的得分步骤展开。

1. Core knowledge

Basic rules

\frac{d}{dx}(x^n)=nx^{n-1},\qquad\frac{d}{dx}(cf)=c\frac{df}{dx},\qquad\frac{d}{dx}(f\pm g)=f'\pm g'.

若 y=f(g(x))，chain rule 为

\frac{dy}{dx}=f'(g(x))g'(x).

Stationary point 满足 dy/dx=0。若 d^2y/dx^2>0 是 minimum，若 d^2y/dx^2<0 是 maximum。

Tangent, normal and rates

点 (x_0,y_0) 处 tangent 的 gradient 是 f'(x_0)：

y-y_0=f'(x_0)(x-x_0).

若 tangent gradient 为 m，normal gradient 为 -1/m。Connected rates of change 使用 chain rule：

\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\frac{dx}{dt}.

做 rates 题时先在指定点求 dy/dx，再代入已知 rate；单位要跟题目保持一致。

Differentiation from first principles

Derivative 的定义是

f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}.

它表示 graph 在该点的 instantaneous gradient。对 composite power function，令 u=ax+b 或 u=f(x)，则

\frac{d}{dx}(ax+b)^n=an(ax+b)^{n-1},\qquad \frac{d}{dx}[f(x)]^n=n[f(x)]^{n-1}f'(x).

遇到 fractional powers，先写成 index form，再使用 power rule 和 chain rule。

Increasing, decreasing and stationary points

在指定 domain 内，dy/dx>0 表示 curve increasing，dy/dx<0 表示 decreasing。Stationary point 由

\frac{dy}{dx}=0

得到；再代回原方程求 coordinates。用 second derivative：d^2y/dx^2>0 为 local minimum，d^2y/dx^2<0 为 local maximum；若 second derivative 等于 0，则必须进一步检查 gradient sign 或其他信息。

在 tangent/normal 题中，先求 tangent gradient m=dy/dx，再使用 normal gradient -1/m；vertical tangent 或 horizontal tangent 时要单独处理。

Connected rates of change

若变量都随时间变化，使用

\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\frac{dx}{dt},\qquad \frac{dV}{dt}=\frac{dV}{dr}\frac{dr}{dt}.

先写出变量之间的 geometric relationship，再对时间 t differentiation，最后只在同一个指定时刻代入数值，并检查 rate 的 units。

2. Verified Paper 1 past-paper questions

Question 1 · 9709/11/M/J/20 Q5(a–b)

The line y=mx+c is tangent to the curve xy=16. (a) Express m in terms of c. (b) Given m=-4, find the set of values of c for which the line intersects the curve at two distinct points.

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Write the curve as y=16/x. Its gradient is

\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{16}{x^2}.

At the contact point x=t, tangent gradient m=-16/t^2, while the tangent intercept is

c=\frac{16}{t}-mt=\frac{16}{t}+\frac{16}{t}=\frac{32}{t}.

Thus t=32/c and

\boxed{m=-\frac{c^2}{64}}.

For m=-4, intersections satisfy

-4x+\!c=\frac{16}{x}\Longrightarrow4x^2-cx+16=0.

Two distinct real roots require c^2-256>0, hence

\boxed{c<-16\text{ or }c>16}.

Question 2 · 9709/11/M/J/20 Q8(a)

The curve is y=\frac8{x+2} and the line is 2y+x=8. They intersect at A and B. Point C lies on the curve and the tangent at C is parallel to AB. Find the coordinates of A,B,C. [6]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

The line is y=4-\frac x2. Equating with the curve:

\frac8{x+2}=4-\frac x2\Longrightarrow x(x-6)=0.

Thus A=(0,4) and B=(6,1). The gradient of AB is -1/2. Since

\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac8{(x+2)^2},

the tangent at C is parallel to AB when

-\frac8{(x+2)^2}=-\frac12\Longrightarrow(x+2)^2=16.

The point shown on the relevant branch is x=2, giving y=2. Hence

\boxed{A=(0,4),\quad B=(6,1),\quad C=(2,2)}.
Source: PastPaper/2020/2020/9709-s20-qp-11.pdf, Q8(a), and matching 9709-s20-ms-11.pdf.

Question 3 · 9709/12/M/J/20 Q3(a–b)

A spherical weather balloon is inflated at a constant volume rate of 600 cm^3 per second. (a) Find its radius after 30 seconds. (b) Find the rate of increase of the radius after 30 seconds.

Show detailed mark scheme answer

After 30 seconds, V=18000. Since V=\frac43\pi r^3,

r^3=\frac{3(18000)}{4\pi}=\frac{13500}{\pi},\qquad\boxed{r=16.3\text{ cm (3 s.f.)}}.

Differentiate with respect to time:

\frac{dV}{dt}=4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}.

At 30 seconds,

\frac{dr}{dt}=\frac{600}{4\pi r^2}=0.1806\ldots.

Therefore

\boxed{\frac{dr}{dt}=0.181\text{ cm s}^{-1}\text{ (3 s.f.)}}.
Source: PastPaper/2020/2020/9709-s20-qp-12.pdf, Q3(a–b), and matching 9709-s20-ms-12.pdf.

Question 4 · 9709/12/M/J/20 Q6(a–b)

The curve is y=2x^2+kx+k-1. (a) Given that y=2x+3 is tangent to the curve, find k. (b) Given k=2, express the curve in completed-square form and state the vertex.

Show detailed mark scheme answer
  1. At an intersection,

2x^2+kx+k-1=2x+3\Longrightarrow2x^2+(k-2)x+k-4=0.

Tangency means discriminant zero:

(k-2)^2-8(k-4)=0 \Longrightarrow k^2-12k+36=0 \Longrightarrow\boxed{k=6}.

  1. With k=2,

y=2x^2+2x+1=2\left(x+\frac12\right)^2+\frac12.

Therefore

\boxed{\text{vertex}=\left(-\frac12,\frac12\right)}.
Source: PastPaper/2020/2020/9709-s20-qp-12.pdf, Q6(a–b), and matching 9709-s20-ms-12.pdf.

Question 5 · 9709/13/M/J/20 Q6(a)

A point moves along y=(5x-1)^{1/2} with dx/dt=1/2 units per minute. Find the rate at which y is increasing when x=1. [4]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

y=(5x-1)^{1/2}\Longrightarrow\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac5{2(5x-1)^{1/2}}.

At x=1,

\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac54.

Using \frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\frac{dx}{dt},

\boxed{\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac54\cdot\frac12=\frac58\text{ units per minute}}.
Source: PastPaper/2020/2020/9709-s20-qp-13.pdf, Q6(a), and matching 9709-s20-ms-13.pdf.

Question 6 · 9709/12/F/M/21 Q6

A curve has \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac6{(3x-2)^3} and passes through A(1,-3). At A, the y-coordinate is increasing at 3 units per second. Find the rate of increase of the x-coordinate. [3]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

At x=1,

\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac6{(3-2)^3}=6.

Therefore

\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\frac{dx}{dt} \Longrightarrow3=6\frac{dx}{dt}.

Hence

\boxed{\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac12\text{ units per second}}.

Question 7 · 9709/12/F/M/22 Q11(a–b)

A curve has equation y=\frac{k}{3x-k}+3x, where k is a constant. Find, in terms of k, the values of x at which there is a stationary point. [4]

  1. The function f has a stationary value at x=a and is defined by f(x)=4(3x-4)^{-1}+3x for x\ge\frac32. Find the value of a and determine the nature of the stationary value. [3]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{3k}{(3x-k)^2}+3.

Set this equal to zero:

-\frac{3k}{(3x-k)^2}+3=0 \Longrightarrow(3x-k)^2=k.

Thus

3x-k=\pm\sqrt{k} \Longrightarrow\boxed{x=\frac{k+\sqrt{k}}3\text{ or }x=\frac{k-\sqrt{k}}3}.

  1. Here k=4, so the possible stationary x-values are 2/3 and 2. The domain x\ge3/2 selects a=2. Also

f''(x)=\frac{72}{(3x-4)^3},

so f''(2)>0. Therefore

\boxed{a=2\text{ and the stationary value is a minimum}.}
Source: PastPaper/2022/2022/9709_m22_qp_12.pdf, Q11(a–b), and matching 9709_m22_ms_12.pdf.

Question 8 · 9709/13/O/N/22 Q4

The water volume is V=243-\frac13(9-x)^3 m^3, where x is depth in metres. Water is pumped in at 3.6 m^3 per hour. Find the rate of increase of depth when x=4, in cm per minute.

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Differentiate:

\frac{dV}{dx}=(9-x)^2.

At x=4, dV/dx=25. Thus

\frac{dV}{dt}=\frac{dV}{dx}\frac{dx}{dt} \Longrightarrow3.6=25\frac{dx}{dt}.

So dx/dt=0.144 m h^{-1}. Convert units:

0.144\text{ m h}^{-1}=\frac{14.4}{60}\text{ cm min}^{-1}.

Therefore

\boxed{\frac{dx}{dt}=0.24\text{ cm min}^{-1}}.
Source: PastPaper/2022/2022/9709_w22_qp_13.pdf, Q4, and matching 9709_w22_ms_13.pdf.

Question 9 · 9709/12/F/M/23 Q10(a–b)

At (4,-1), the gradient is -3/2. It is given that \frac{dy}{dx}=x^{-1/2}+k. (a) Show k=-2. (b) Find the equation of the curve.

Show detailed mark scheme answer

At x=4,

-\frac32=4^{-1/2}+k=\frac12+k\Longrightarrow\boxed{k=-2}.

Integrate:

y=\int(x^{-1/2}-2)\,dx=2x^{1/2}-2x+C.

Use (4,-1):

-1=2(2)-8+C\Longrightarrow C=3.

Hence

\boxed{y=2\sqrt{x}-2x+3}.
Source: PastPaper/2023/2023/9709_m23_qp_12.pdf, Q10(a–b), and matching 9709_m23_ms_12.pdf.

Question 10 · 9709/11/O/N/23 Q10(a–d)

A curve has a stationary point at (2,-10) and \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=6x.

  1. Find dy/dx. [3]

  2. Find the equation of the curve. [3]

  3. Find the coordinates of the other stationary point and determine its nature. [3]

  4. Find the equation of the tangent to the curve at the point where it crosses the y-axis. [2]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Integrate the second derivative:

\frac{dy}{dx}=3x^2+C.

At the stationary point x=2, dy/dx=0:

0=3(2)^2+C\Longrightarrow C=-12.

Therefore

\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=3x^2-12}.

  1. Integrating,

y=x^3-12x+C.

Using (2,-10) gives C=6, so \boxed{y=x^3-12x+6}.

  1. Stationary points satisfy 3x^2-12=0, so x=\pm2. At x=-2, y=22. Since d^2y/dx^2=6x<0 there,

\boxed{(-2,22)\text{, a maximum point}}.

  1. At the y-axis, (x,y)=(0,6) and the gradient is -12. Hence the tangent is
\boxed{y=-12x+6}.
Source: PastPaper/2023/2023/9709_w23_qp_11.pdf, Q10(a–d), and matching 9709_w23_ms_11.pdf.

Question 11 · 9709/12/M/J/24 Q6(a–b)

The curve is y=2x-8x^{1/2}. It has a minimum point A and intersects the positive x-axis at B. Find the coordinates of A and B. [4]

  1. Find the area of the shaded region between the curve and the line y=\frac{2x-32}{3}. [5]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{dx}=2-4x^{-1/2}.

At a stationary point,

2-\frac4{\sqrt{x}}=0\Longrightarrow\sqrt{x}=2\Longrightarrow x=4.

Then y=8-16=-8, so A=(4,-8). For the positive intercept,

2x-8\sqrt{x}=0\Longrightarrow\sqrt{x}(2\sqrt{x}-8)=0,

giving x=16. Therefore

\boxed{A=(4,-8),\qquad B=(16,0)}.

  1. The curve and line meet at x=4 and x=16. Between them the curve is above the line, so
\begin{aligned} \text{area}&=\int_4^{16}\left[2x-8\sqrt{x}-\frac{2x-32}{3}\right]dx\\ &=\boxed{\frac{32}{3}}. \end{aligned}
Source: PastPaper/2024/2024/qp-202406-mathematics-p12.pdf, Q6(a–b), and matching ms-202406-mathematics-p12.pdf.

Question 13 · 9709/13/M/J/24 Q5(a–c)

The curve is y=2x^2-\frac5{2x}+3. (a) Find the stationary point. (b) Determine its nature. (c) For positive x, determine whether the curve is increasing, decreasing or neither.

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{dx}=4x+\frac5{2x^2}.

Set equal to zero:

8x^3+5=0\Longrightarrow x=-\frac{\sqrt[3]{5}}2.

At this value, with x^3=-5/8, the y-coordinate is

2x^2-\frac5{2x}+3=3+\frac32\sqrt[3]{25}.

Also

\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=4-\frac5{x^3}=12>0

at the stationary point. Hence

\boxed{\left(-\frac{\sqrt[3]{5}}2,\ 3+\frac32\sqrt[3]{25}\right)\text{ is a minimum}}.

For x>0, both terms in dy/dx=4x+5/(2x^2) are positive, so the curve is

\boxed{\text{increasing for all }x>0}.
Source: PastPaper/2024/2024/qp-202406-mathematics-p13.pdf, Q5(a–c), and matching ms-202406-mathematics-p13.pdf.

Question 14 · 9709/11/O/N/24 Q2

The curve y=x^2-\frac ax has a stationary point at (-3,b). Find a and b. [4]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{dx}=2x+\frac a{x^2}.

At x=-3,

0=-6+\frac a9\Longrightarrow a=54.

Then

b=(-3)^2-\frac{54}{-3}=9+18=27.

Therefore

\boxed{a=54,\qquad b=27}.

Question 15 · 9709/11/O/N/24 Q5(a–b)

The derivative of a curve is \frac{dy}{dx}=4x-3\sqrt{x}+1.

  1. Find the x-coordinate of the point on the curve at which the gradient is 11/2. [3]

  2. Given that the curve passes through (4,11), find the equation of the curve. [4]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Set the derivative equal to the required gradient:

4x-3\sqrt{x}+1=\frac{11}{2}.

Let u=\sqrt{x}. Then x=u^2 and

8u^2-6u-9=0=(4u+3)(2u-3).

Since u\ge0, u=3/2. Hence

\boxed{x=\left(\frac32\right)^2=\frac94}.

  1. Integrating,

y=2x^2-2x^{3/2}+x+C.

Using (4,11) gives C=-9, so

\boxed{y=2x^2-2x^{3/2}+x-9}.
Source: PastPaper/2024/2024/qp-202410-mathematics-p11.pdf, Q5(a–b), and matching ms-202410-mathematics-p11.pdf.

Question 16 · 9709/12/O/N/24 Q3(a–b)

The curve is y=2x^2-3. Points A and B have x-coordinates 2 and 2+h. (a) Find the gradient of chord AB in terms of h. (b) Explain how the gradient at A follows from your answer.

Show detailed mark scheme answer

y_A=2(2)^2-3=5,\qquad y_B=2(2+h)^2-3.

The chord gradient is

\frac{y_B-y_A}{(2+h)-2} =\frac{2(2+h)^2-3-5}{h} =\boxed{8+2h}.

As h\to0, the second point approaches A, so the chord gradient tends to the tangent gradient:

\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}\bigg|_{x=2}=8}.
Source: PastPaper/2024/2024/qp-202410-mathematics-p12.pdf, Q3(a–b), and matching ms-202410-mathematics-p12.pdf.
