8. Differential equations

Separation of variables · initial conditions · mathematical models

CIE 9709 Paper 3 Pure Mathematics 3, syllabus 3.8.
重要本节考纲要求

本页依据 9709 Mathematics 2026–2027 syllabus 3.8：把简单 rate-of-change statement 写成 differential equation；用 separation of variables 和 integration 解一阶 differential equation；使用 initial condition 求 particular solution；解释解在 model 中的含义。

本章的 P3 note 文件夹没有独立的 Differential equations 笔记 PDF，因此这里不添加未经来源支持的 note 图片；知识点用公式卡呈现，真题题干直接按 past paper 保留。

1. Core knowledge

Separation of variables

若

\frac{dy}{dx}=F(x)G(y),

先把 y 的因子与 dy 放在一边，把 x 的因子与 dx 放在另一边，再分别积分：

\frac{dy}{G(y)}=F(x)\,dx.

积分常数必须在使用 initial condition 后确定。若最后需要 y，还要根据 initial condition 选择正确的 branch。

Knowledge-point example · particular solution

若

\frac{dy}{dx}=x e^{y-x},\qquad y=0\text{ when }x=0,

则

e^{-y}\,dy=xe^{-x}\,dx \Longrightarrow -e^{-y}=-(x+1)e^{-x}+C.

代入 (x,y)=(0,0) 得 C=0，所以

\boxed{y=x-\ln(x+1)}.

Model interpretation

比例关系必须先写出比例常数，例如

\frac{dx}{dt}\propto x(300-x) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dx}{dt}=k x(300-x).

再用一个已知的 rate value 求 k。最后的 particular solution 要满足 initial condition，并且应在题目给出的物理范围内取值。

Separable equations in x and y

Question 1 · 9709/32/M/J/20 Q7

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y-1}{(x+1)(x+3)}.

It is given that y=2 when x=0.

Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for y in terms of x. [9]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{y-1}=\frac{dx}{(x+1)(x+3)} =\frac12\left(\frac1{x+1}-\frac1{x+3}\right)dx.

因此

\ln|y-1|=\frac12\ln\left|\frac{x+1}{x+3}\right|+C.

由 y=2 when x=0，并且 y-1>0：

y-1=\sqrt{\frac{3(x+1)}{x+3}}, \qquad \boxed{y=1+\sqrt{\frac{3(x+1)}{x+3}}}.
Source: PastPaper/2020/2020/9709-s20-qp-32.pdf, Q7, and matching mark scheme.

Question 2 · 9709/32/O/N/20 Q7

The variables x and t satisfy the differential equation

e^{3t}\frac{dx}{dt}=\cos^2 2x,

for t\ge0. It is given that x=0 when t=0.

  1. Solve the differential equation and obtain an expression for x in terms of t. [7]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

\sec^2(2x)\,dx=e^{-3t}\,dt.

积分得

\frac12\tan(2x)=-\frac13e^{-3t}+C.

代入 x=0 when t=0 得 C=1/3，所以

\tan(2x)=\frac23(1-e^{-3t}).

由于 x=0 时取 principal branch，

\boxed{x=\frac12\tan^{-1}\left(\frac23(1-e^{-3t})\right)}.
Source: PastPaper/2020/2020/9709_w20_qp_32.pdf, Q7, and matching mark scheme.

Question 3 · 9709/32/F/M/21 Q4

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

(1-\cos x)\frac{dy}{dx}=y\sin x.

It is given that y=4 when x=\pi.

  1. Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for y in terms of x. [6]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac1y\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{\sin x}{1-\cos x} =\frac{d}{dx}\ln(1-\cos x).

因此 y=C(1-\cos x)。由 y=4 when x=\pi：

4=C(1-(-1))=2C\Longrightarrow C=2.

故

\boxed{y=2(1-\cos x)}.
Source: PastPaper/2021/2021/9709_m21_qp_32.pdf, Q4, and matching mark scheme.

Question 4 · 9709/31/M/J/21 Q10

The variables x and t satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dx}{dt}=x^2(1+2x),

and x=1 when t=0.

Using partial fractions, solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for t in terms of x. [11]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dt}{dx}=\frac1{x^2(1+2x)} =-\frac2x+\frac1{x^2}+\frac4{1+2x}.

积分并使用 t=0 when x=1：

t=-2\ln x-\frac1x+2\ln(1+2x)+1-2\ln3.

整理为

\boxed{t=2\ln\left(\frac{1+2x}{3x}\right)+1-\frac1x}.
Source: PastPaper/2021/2021/9709_s21_qp_31.pdf, Q10, and matching mark scheme.

Question 5 · 9709/32/O/N/21 Q7

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

e^{2x}\frac{dy}{dx}=4xy^2,

and it is given that y=1 when x=0.

Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for y in terms of x. [7]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

y^{-2}\,dy=4xe^{-2x}\,dx.

Since

\int4xe^{-2x}dx=-(2x+1)e^{-2x},

we have

-\frac1y=-(2x+1)e^{-2x}+C.

At (x,y)=(0,1), C=0. Hence

\boxed{y=\frac{e^{2x}}{2x+1}}.
Source: PastPaper/2021/2021/9709_w21_qp_32.pdf, Q7, and matching mark scheme.

Question 6 · 9709/32/F/M/22 Q9

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

(x+1)(3x+1)\frac{dy}{dx}=y,

and it is given that y=1 when x=1.

Solve the differential equation and find the exact value of y when x=3, giving your answer in a simplified form. [9]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{y}=\frac{dx}{(x+1)(3x+1)},qquad \frac1{(x+1)(3x+1)}=-\frac1{2(x+1)}+\frac3{2(3x+1)}.

Thus

\ln y=\frac12\ln\left(\frac{3x+1}{x+1}\right)+C.

Using y=1 at x=1 gives C=\frac12\ln2, so

y=\sqrt{\frac{2(3x+1)}{x+1}}.

At x=3,

\boxed{y=\sqrt5}.
Source: PastPaper/2022/2022/9709_m22_qp_32.pdf, Q9, and matching mark scheme.

Question 7 · 9709/31/M/J/22 Q4

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{xy}{1+x^2},

and y=2 when x=0.

Solve the differential equation, obtaining a simplified expression for y in terms of x. [7]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{y}=\frac{x}{1+x^2}\,dx \Longrightarrow \ln y=\frac12\ln(1+x^2)+C.

At x=0,y=2, C=\ln2. Hence

\boxed{y=2\sqrt{1+x^2}}.

Question 8 · 9709/32/M/J/22 Q6

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dy}{dx}=xe^{y-x},

and y=0 when x=0.

  1. Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for y in terms of x. [7]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

e^{-y}\,dy=xe^{-x}\,dx.

Integrating,

-e^{-y}=-(x+1)e^{-x}+C.

The initial condition gives C=0, so

e^{-y}=(x+1)e^{-x} \Longrightarrow \boxed{y=x-\ln(x+1)}.

Question 9 · 9709/32/O/N/22 Q7(a–b)

The variables x and \theta satisfy the differential equation

x\sin^2\theta\frac{dx}{d\theta}=\tan^2\theta-2\cot\theta,

for 0<\theta<\frac12\pi and x>0. It is given that x=2 when \theta=\frac14\pi.

  1. Show that

\frac{d}{d\theta}(\cot^2\theta)=-\frac{2\cot\theta}{\sin^2\theta}.

(You may assume without proof that the derivative of \cot\theta with respect to \theta is -\cosec^2\theta.)

  1. Solve the differential equation and find the value of x when \theta=\frac16\pi. [1+7]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{d}{d\theta}(\cot^2\theta)=2\cot\theta(-\cosec^2\theta) =-\frac{2\cot\theta}{\sin^2\theta}.

  1. Divide by \sin^2\theta:

x\frac{dx}{d\theta}=\sec^2\theta-\frac{2\cot\theta}{\sin^2\theta} =\frac{d}{d\theta}\left(\tan\theta+\cot^2\theta\right).

Thus

\frac12x^2=\tan\theta+\cot^2\theta+C.

At \theta=\pi/4,x=2, C=0. Therefore

x^2=2(\tan\theta+\cot^2\theta).

At \theta=\pi/6,

\boxed{x=\sqrt{6+\frac2{\sqrt3}}}.
Source: PastPaper/2022/2022/9709_w22_qp_32.pdf, Q7(a–b), and matching mark scheme.

Question 10 · 9709/31/M/J/23 Q7

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\cos 2x\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{4\tan 2x}{\sin^2 3y},

where 0\le x<\frac14\pi. It is given that y=0 when x=\frac16\pi.

Solve the differential equation to obtain the value of x when y=\frac16\pi. Give your answer correct to 3 decimal places. [8]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Separate and integrate:

\sin^2(3y)\,dy=\frac{4\sin2x}{\cos^2 2x}\,dx.

Hence

\frac y2-\frac{\sin6y}{12}=2\sec2x+C.

At (x,y)=(\pi/6,0), C=-4. When y=\pi/6,

\frac\pi{12}=2\sec2x-4 \Longrightarrow \sec2x=2+\frac\pi{24}.

Thus

x=\frac12\cos^{-1}\left(\frac1{2+\pi/24}\right) \approx\boxed{0.568}.
Source: PastPaper/2023/2023/9709_s23_qp_31.pdf, Q7, and matching mark scheme.

Question 11 · 9709/32/M/J/23 Q8(a)

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{4+9y^2}{e^{2x+1}}.

It is given that y=0 when x=1.

Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for y in terms of x. [7]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{4+9y^2}=e^{-(2x+1)}dx.

Integrating and using y=0 when x=1 gives

\frac16\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{3y}{2}\right) =-\frac12e^{-(2x+1)}+\frac12e^{-3}.

Therefore

\boxed{y=\frac23\tan\left(3e^{-3}-3e^{-(2x+1)}\right)}.

Question 12 · 9709/33/M/J/23 Q8

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y^2+4}{x(y+4)}

for x>0. It is given that x=4 when y=2\sqrt3.

Solve the differential equation to obtain the value of x when y=2. [8]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Write the equation as

\frac{dx}{x}=\frac{y+4}{y^2+4}\,dy.

Then

\ln x=\frac12\ln(y^2+4)+2\tan^{-1}\left(\frac y2\right)+C.

At x=4,y=2\sqrt3, C=-2\pi/3. At y=2,

\ln x=\frac32\ln2-\frac\pi6,

so

\boxed{x=2\sqrt2\,e^{-\pi/6}}.
Source: PastPaper/2023/2023/9709_s23_qp_33.pdf, Q8, and matching mark scheme.

Question 13 · 9709/32/F/M/23 Q9

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{3y}\sin^2 2x.

It is given that y=0 when x=0.

Solve the differential equation and find the value of y when x=\frac12. [7]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

e^{-3y}dy=\sin^2 2x\,dx.

Using \sin^2 2x=(1-\cos4x)/2,

-\frac13e^{-3y}=\frac x2-\frac{\sin4x}{8}+C.

The initial condition gives C=-1/3, hence

e^{-3y}=1-\frac32x+\frac38\sin4x.

At x=1/2,

\boxed{y=-\frac13\ln\left(\frac14+\frac38\sin2\right)}.

Question 14 · 9709/31/O/N/23 Q7

The variables x and \phi satisfy the differential equation

\frac{x}{\tan\phi}\frac{dx}{d\phi}=x^2+3.

It is given that x=1 when \phi=0.

Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for x^2 in terms of \phi. [7]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{x}{x^2+3}\,dx=\tan\phi\,d\phi.

Integrating,

\frac12\ln(x^2+3)=-\ln(\cos\phi)+C.

At (x,\phi)=(1,0), C=\ln2. Therefore

\ln(x^2+3)=\ln(4\sec^2\phi), \qquad \boxed{x^2=4\sec^2\phi-3}.
Source: PastPaper/2023/2023/9709_w23_qp_31.pdf, Q7, and matching mark scheme.

Question 15 · 9709/32/O/N/23 Q11(a)

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

x^2\frac{dy}{dx}+y^2+y=0.

It is given that x=1 when y=1.

  1. Solve the differential equation to obtain an expression for y in terms of x. [8]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

\frac{dy}{y(y+1)}=-\frac{dx}{x^2}, \qquad \frac1{y(y+1)}=\frac1y-\frac1{y+1}.

Thus

\ln\left|\frac y{y+1}\right|=\frac1x+C.

At (x,y)=(1,1), C=-1-\ln2. Hence

\frac y{y+1}=\frac12e^{1/x-1}.

Solving for y gives

\boxed{y=\frac{e^{1/x-1}}{2-e^{1/x-1}}}.
Source: PastPaper/2023/2023/9709_w23_qp_32.pdf, Q11(a), and matching mark scheme.

Question 16 · 9709/33/O/N/23 Q8

The variables x and y satisfy the differential equation

e^{4x}\frac{dy}{dx}=\cos^2 3y.

It is given that y=0 when x=2.

Solve the differential equation, obtaining an expression for y in terms of x. [7]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

\sec^2(3y)\,dy=e^{-4x}\,dx.

After integration,

\frac13\tan3y=-\frac14e^{-4x}+C.

Using y=0 at x=2 gives C=e^{-8}/4. Thus

\boxed{y=\frac13\tan^{-1}\left(\frac34(e^{-8}-e^{-4x})\right)}.

Question 17 · 9709/32/F/M/24 Q11

The variables y and i satisfy the differential equation

(1+y)(1+\cos2i)\frac{dy}{di}=e^{3y}.

It is given that y=0 when i=\frac14\pi.

Solve the differential equation and find the exact value of \tan i when y=1. [9]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

(1+y)e^{-3y}dy=\frac{di}{1+\cos2i}=\frac12\sec^2i\,di.

Since

\int(1+y)e^{-3y}dy=-\frac{(3y+4)e^{-3y}}9,

the initial condition gives

-\frac{(3y+4)e^{-3y}}9=\frac12\tan i-\frac{17}{18}.

At y=1,

\boxed{\tan i=\frac{17-14e^{-3}}9}.

Question 18 · 9709/31/M/J/24 Q11

In a field there are 300 plants of a certain species, all of which can be infected by a particular disease. At time t after the first plant is infected there are x infected plants. The rate of change of x is proportional to the product of the number of plants infected and the number of plants that are not yet infected. The variables x and t are treated as continuous, and it is given that \frac{dx}{dt}=0.2 and x=1 when t=0.

  1. Show that x and t satisfy the differential equation

1495\frac{dx}{dt}=x(300-x).

  1. Using partial fractions, solve the differential equation and obtain an expression for t in terms of a single logarithm involving x. [2+9]
Show detailed mark scheme answer

At x=1, the product is 1(299), so 0.2=k(299) and hence k=1/1495, proving (a).

For (b),

\frac{1495\,dx}{x(300-x)}=dt, \qquad \frac1{x(300-x)}=\frac1{300}\left(\frac1x+\frac1{300-x}\right).

Using x=1 when t=0,

\frac{1495}{300}\ln\left(\frac{x}{300-x}\right) -\frac{1495}{300}\ln\left(\frac1{299}\right)=t.

Therefore

\boxed{t=\frac{1495}{300}\ln\left(\frac{299x}{300-x}\right)}.
Source: PastPaper/2024/2024/A-Level-CAIE数学QP-202406-Mathematics-P31-A-Level题库大全小程序.pdf, Q11, and matching mark scheme.

Question 19 · 9709/32/O/N/24 Q10

A balloon in the shape of a sphere has volume V and radius r. Air is pumped into the balloon at a constant rate of 40\pi starting when time t=0 and r=0. At the same time, air begins to flow out of the balloon at a rate of 0.8\pi r. The balloon remains a sphere at all times.

  1. Show that r and t satisfy the differential equation

\frac{dr}{dt}=\frac{50-r}{5r^2}.

  1. Find the quotient and remainder when 5r^2 is divided by 50-r.

  2. Solve the differential equation in part (a), obtaining an expression for t in terms of r. [3+3+6]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

Since V=\frac43\pi r^3, dV/dt=4\pi r^2\,dr/dt. Equating net volume rate 40\pi-0.8\pi r gives (a).

For (b),

\frac{5r^2}{50-r}=-5r-250+\frac{12500}{50-r}.

Therefore

\begin{aligned} t&=\int_0^r\frac{5s^2}{50-s}\,ds\\ &=\left[-\frac52s^2-250s+12500\ln\left(\frac{50}{50-s}\right)\right]_0^r. \end{aligned}

Hence

\boxed{t=-\frac52r^2-250r+12500\ln\left(\frac{50}{50-r}\right)}.
Source: PastPaper/2024/2024/A-Level-CAIE数学QP-202410-Mathematics-P32-A-Level题库大全小程序.pdf, Q10, and matching mark scheme.

Question 20 · 9709/33/O/N/22 Q10

A gardener is filling an ornamental pool with water, using a hose that delivers 30 litres of water per minute. Initially the pool is empty. At time t minutes after filling begins the volume of water in the pool is V litres. The pool has a small leak and loses water at a rate of 0.01V litres per minute.

\frac{dV}{dt}=a-bV.

  1. Write down the values of the constants a and b.

  2. Solve the differential equation and find the value of t when V=1000. [1+6]

Show detailed mark scheme answer

From the rates, \boxed{a=30} and \boxed{b=0.01}.

The equation is

\frac{dV}{dt}=30-0.01V.

Separate and use V=0 when t=0:

\frac{dV}{3000-V}=0.01\,dt \Longrightarrow -\ln(3000-V)=0.01t+C.

Hence

V=3000(1-e^{-0.01t}).

When V=1000,

e^{-0.01t}=\frac23 \Longrightarrow \boxed{t=100\ln\frac32\text{ minutes}}.
Source: PastPaper/2022/2022/9709_w22_qp_33.pdf, Q10, and matching mark scheme.

Exam checklist

  1. 分离变量时不要漏掉 dy/dx 中的 dx 或把 e^{-x} 移错边。
  2. 先积分，再使用 initial condition；积分常数通常决定最终 branch。
  3. 三角积分要同时检查区间和 inverse-trigonometric branch。
  4. 应用题先从 proportionality 写出常数，再用 given rate 求常数。
  5. 最后把答案代回 differential equation 和 initial condition 检查。
